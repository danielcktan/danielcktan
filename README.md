# Hi, I’m Daniel 👋
### I am a Web Designer & Front-end Developer

I enjoy turning ideas into modern, user-friendly and accessible websites.

My work brings together design, front-end development and user experience. I like getting to the heart of a problem, finding a practical way forward, and then refining the details that make a website easier and more enjoyable to use.

This GitHub profile is where I share selected projects, experiments and code that I can openly showcase.

## What Matters To Me

I believe good digital experiences should make things easier, not harder.

I care about the details that help people understand information, find what they need and feel confident using a website.

That means thinking about:

* **People** – Designing for real needs, abilities and contexts
* **Clarity** – Keeping information and interactions easy to understand
* **Inclusion** – Considering accessibility from the beginning, not as an afterthought
* **Quality** – Paying attention to the details that improve an experience
* **Purpose** – Making sure design choices serve a real need
* **Sustainability** – Building things that can be maintained and improved over time

## What I Do

My work brings together design, front-end development and digital technology.

* 🎨 **Web & UI Design** – Creating clear and purposeful interfaces
* 💻 **Front-end Development** – Building responsive websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript
* 🧩 **CMS Development** – Building and customising websites with Joomla and WordPress
* ⚡ **Digital Experiences** – Connecting design, content and technology
* 📱 **Responsive Design** – Creating solutions that adapt across devices
* 🤖 **AI-assisted Workflows** – Exploring AI as part of the design and development process

I enjoy moving between the creative and technical sides of a project, from shaping an idea and designing the interface to building it and refining the details.
